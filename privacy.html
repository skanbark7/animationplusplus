Privacy Policy

How we handle data across our educational platform, APIs, and 3D simulations.

Last Updated: October 2026 • Animation++ Technologies, Pune, Maharashtra, India

1. Commitment to Student & Institutional Privacy

Animation++ Technologies ("Animation++", "we", "our") is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy of educational institutions, teachers, and students. We align our data processing with the India Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA).

2. Zero Student Data Sale Guarantee

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker student personal information to advertisers, data brokers, or third-party marketing companies under any circumstances. Telemetry collected inside our 3D WebGL simulations (such as rotation angles or session completion) is strictly processed to evaluate conceptual retention and improve simulation ergonomics.

3. Institutional Curriculum Ingestion

When educational institutions upload proprietary lecture notes, textbook syllabi, or exam blueprints to our curriculum deconstruction engine:

  • Data is processed in isolated enterprise computing environments.
  • Curriculum files are never utilized to train public foundation models without explicit written permission.
  • Partner institutions retain 100% ownership over all uploaded educational materials.

4. Cloud Infrastructure & Security Standards

Our simulation compilation engine utilizes enterprise cloud infrastructure, including Microsoft Azure OpenAI, ElevenLabs, and fal.ai. All API calls utilize zero-data-retention agreements, TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, and AES-256 encryption at rest.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Educational institutions may request complete deletion of their account data, uploaded curriculum outlines, and generated simulation assets at any time by emailing our privacy desk. All requests are processed within 14 business days.
