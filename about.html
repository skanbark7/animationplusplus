OUR STORY

Why We Started in Pune, India

India has the largest concentration of high-school and university STEM students in the world.

01

The Rote Memorization Trap

Every year, millions of students prepare for competitive STEM exams like JEE and NEET. Most are forced to memorize textbook pages because their schools lack 3D visualization tools.

02

The 3D Production Bottleneck

Creating scientific 3D animations used to cost thousands of dollars per minute and take weeks of manual animator time. Our engine automates this pipeline down to under two minutes.

03

Empirical Validation First

We did not start with an enterprise sales pitch. We validated every visual model with 565,000+ real students first to make sure our 3D modules actually improve conceptual test scores.
