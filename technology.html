The 4-Tier Generative 3D Engine

Building a 3D scientific simulation used to take weeks of manual work in Blender. Our pipeline takes raw syllabus text, finds where students get stuck, and generates real-time WebGL models with synchronized voice narration in under two minutes.

FOUNDATION ARCHITECTURE

Four Specialized Pipeline Tiers

Each tier handles a distinct computational challenge: curriculum parsing, voice synthesis, visual diffusion, and client-side 3D rendering.

TIER 01
🧠

Curriculum Deconstruction

Azure OpenAI (GPT-4o) Claude 3.5 Sonnet

Ingests full textbook chapters and syllabus guides (CBSE, ICSE, AP, IB). Maps the core concept hierarchy and identifies the exact step where students lose track of the mechanism.

TIER 02
🎙️

Neural Narration Layer

ElevenLabs Multilingual Audio

Generates spoken narration in clear English and conversational bilingual Hinglish. Calibrated to match the visual pacing of the 3D model, with accurate pronunciation of scientific terms.

TIER 03
⚡

Visual & Motion Diffusion

fal.ai Cloud Compute FLUX.1 Kling AI

Uses fast serverless inference via fal.ai. Generates high-detail anatomical cutaways with FLUX.1 and dynamic motion physics simulations with Kling AI for continuous visual reference.

TIER 04
📐

Spatial Geometry Compiler

WebGL 2.0 Three.js PBR Runtime

Compiles mathematical formulas and spatial trajectories into interactive 3D particle meshes and vector fields that students can rotate and inspect at 60 FPS on any phone or laptop.
